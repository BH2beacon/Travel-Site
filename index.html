All About Paris


Paris is the largest city in France, as well as its capital. It has been around for many centuries, having started as one of many expansions of the Roman empire, and has since become one of the most well-recognized cities in the world. Paris is the capital of France, and one of the most dazzling of places to see both at daytime and nighttime, which is why it is known by many as the City of Light, With bedazzling landmarks such as the Louvre Museum, the Notre-Dame cathedral, and the Eiffel Tower, you are certain to have an Unforgettable experience when visiting this beautiful city.


Reasons to Visit

  • Beautiful landscapes
  • Exquisite foods
  • Unforgettable sights
  • Historical landmarks

What To Do in Paris


There are many things you can do when visiting Paris. One of these, of course, is to see the Eiffel Tower, a national landmark that has been standing since 1889, and a destination you will want to go to at least once in a lifetime! It has exhibitions of the tower's history, restaurants, gift shops, and magnificent views of Paris all around.


Another location worth visiting is the Louvre, which is one of the most exquisite art museums in Paris. Over 230 years old, older than even the Eiffel Tower, this museum has stood the test of time hosting many art pieces such as paintings, sculptures, and even relics from ancient Greece, Egypt, and Rome.


Finally, one last location that is recommended is the cathedral of Notre-Dame. Built in 1163 and consecrated in 1182, this medieval building is a popular attraction that is very well known amongst religious enthusiasts. It was even the subject of a story written in 1831 titled, “The Hunchback of Notre-Dame,” as a means to promote the restoration of the building in between the years 1844 and 1864. The building has gone through many damages and restorations over the centuries, with one of the most recent examples being in 2019 when a fire broke out in the cathedral that destroyed the roof and the spires. It took them three years to repair the damage and was reopened in December of 2024.

These, of course, are only three things I would recommend you do when in Paris; there are so much more things you can do, and many places you can visit over there. There are many museums that showcase important relics history left behind, as well as many bakeries, movie theaters, a replica of the Statue of Liberty, and even a Disneyland park.
